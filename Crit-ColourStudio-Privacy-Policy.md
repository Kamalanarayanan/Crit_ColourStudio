# Privacy Policy — Crit ColourStudio

**Effective date:** 20 August 2026  
**Last updated:** 20 August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Crit ColourStudio, shown in the app as Colour
Studio (the “App”). The App is designed and built by Kamalanarayanan as a CRIT
Studio product.

## Summary

The App is designed to work without an account. It does not send personal data
to Kamalanarayanan or CRIT Studio, and it does not use advertising, analytics,
cross-app tracking or third-party software development kits. Learning records,
feedback and optional evidence photos are processed and stored on the device.
They leave the device only when the user deliberately shares an export through
Apple's system share sheet.

## Information processed on the device

### Learning and app records

The App may save the following information in its local app storage:

- lesson progress, completion dates and exercise scores;
- pinned colour references and app setup choices;
- notebook entries and reflections;
- test-feedback ratings, categories and comments; and
- device model, iOS version and app version, but only when the user chooses to
  include this context in a feedback note.

These records are used only to provide the App's learning, notebook and testing
features. They are not transmitted to the developer.

### Camera

With the user's permission, Camera Colour Hunt and the Digital Glitch exercise
use the rear camera to sample rendered colour, compare white balance and find a
nearby paper swatch. Camera frames are analysed live on the device. The App does
not record or upload those frames as photographs or video.

### Optional evidence photos

The user may choose a photograph through Apple's system photo picker as
evidence of a physical exercise. The App receives only the image selected by
the user. It prepares a smaller JPEG and stores it in the App's private
Application Support directory on the device. The photograph is used to unlock
locally generated reward artwork. It is not uploaded, reviewed by an instructor
or used to verify an external credential.

### Apple Intelligence

On compatible devices, the optional Studio Critic uses Apple's on-device
Foundation Models framework to explain exercise results. The App provides the
model with the current exercise context and colour metrics. This processing
does not use a CRIT Studio server, and the developer does not receive the prompt
or response. The App's core exercises remain available when Apple Intelligence
is unavailable.

## Exports and sharing

The App can create feedback summaries, notebook cards, certificates, images and
short videos on the device. An export is shared only after the user opens
Apple's system share sheet and chooses a destination. The selected destination,
such as Mail, Messages, Files or a social platform, may process the exported
content under its own privacy policy. The App does not control those services.

Users should review exported material before sharing it and should not include
sensitive information unless they intend to send it to the selected recipient
or service.

## Data collection, tracking and advertising

Kamalanarayanan and CRIT Studio do not collect data from the App. In particular,
the App does not:

- create user accounts;
- collect contact, location, health, payment or advertising data;
- use analytics or advertising services;
- track users across apps or websites;
- sell personal information; or
- send app activity or user-created content to a developer-operated server.

Apple may process information when the user uses iOS services such as the photo
picker, system share sheet or Apple Intelligence. Apple's handling of that
information is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Permissions and user choices

Camera access is requested only when the user opens a camera-based exercise.
The permission can be changed at any time in iOS Settings. Photo access occurs
through Apple's system photo picker and is limited to the photograph the user
selects.

Uploading evidence is optional. A physical exercise can instead be marked as
complete without supplying a photograph. Including device context in a
feedback note is also optional.

## Storage, retention and deletion

Local records remain on the device so that progress and saved work are
available between sessions.

- Feedback notes can be deleted individually or all at once inside the App.
- Notebook entries can be deleted individually inside the App.
- Pinned colour references can be removed or cleared inside the App.
- An evidence photograph remains in private app storage until it is replaced by
  another photograph for the same exercise or the App and its data are deleted.
  Version 1.0 does not provide a separate delete-evidence control.
- Other progress and setup records remain until the App and its data are
  deleted.

To remove all locally stored information, use **Delete App** in iOS Settings or
from the Home Screen. Offloading the App may retain its documents and data, so
use Delete App when complete removal is intended.

Because the App does not transmit this information to the developer, there is
no developer-held account or cloud record to delete. For privacy questions or
assistance, use the contact address below.

## Security

Local records and evidence photographs are kept within the App's iOS sandbox.
No method of storage is completely secure, so users should also protect access
to their device and avoid sharing sensitive material through third-party
destinations.

## Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other
users because it does not transmit user information to the developer. A parent,
guardian or school supervising a child's device should review any material the
child chooses to export to another service.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the App's data practices change. The effective
date and last-updated date at the top of this page will identify the current
version. App Store privacy information will also be updated when required.
